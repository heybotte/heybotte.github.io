Hive privacy policy

Hive reads and changes Gmail, Calendar and Drive data only in the Google account that authorized it.

That data stays on its owner's computer. It is not sold, shared with anyone, or used for advertising.

Hive's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

To revoke access, remove Hive at myaccount.google.com → Security → Third-party access.
